`public/bundle/` contains project-authored, aggregate deployment artifacts for
the Rhinoform research prototype. They include transformed browser parameters,
population statistics, conformance references and a training-mean display
context. The project author has elected to publish these artifacts with the
demo. They do not contain an original or single-identity FaceScape scan and do
not grant access to, or rights in, the FaceScape dataset.

independently from the [official provider](https://nju-3dv.github.io/projects/FaceScape/)
